# Research records

The [project README](../../README.md) and [four-page report](../../report/progress_report.pdf) describe the current project. This directory keeps the analyses, literature notes and earlier plans behind them.

- [rock_6 frame-score diagnostic](rock6_third_diagnostic_2026-11-16/README.md): frozen predictions, local quality margins and the priority of decoder calibration.
- [Recent literature](recent_chord_literature_2026-11-15/review.md): targeted reading through November 15, with paper versions, reading depth and six-field source records.
- [Paired errors and annotation checks](root_questions_2026-11-13/report_design.md): the initial analysis that motivated the revised question.
- [Root-error experiments](root_optimization/README.md): development comparisons and reproduction instructions for isolated chords and fast-tempo recordings.
- [Original error analysis](error_analysis/README.md): GuitarSet and slow-tempo IDMT results.

Dated plans and language reviews record decisions at the time they were written. Later plans may supersede their priorities; original measurements, configurations and source snapshots are retained. Scripts with absolute session paths are archived checks; use the linked reproduction instructions on a new machine.

Literature records link to the original publications. Local PDF names in those records identify the copies used for reading; those copies are not distributed here. Reading depth and unresolved source questions remain as documented in each record.
